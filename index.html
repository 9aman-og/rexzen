01First focus

people can use.

Our proposed first focus is AI that helps people turn knowledge into useful work. The first product will begin with a specific problem, an outcome we can demonstrate, and people who need it.

Rexzen is at the beginning. We’re defining that focus before announcing a product.

02The wider horizon

One company.
Room to explore.

AI is our starting point. These are the directions we want to earn the ability to explore over time.

IArtificial intelligenceStarting focus

Tools and systems that help people reason, plan, and build. Our first task is to choose a focused use case and make it work well.

IINew computing systemsLonger-term ambition

Neuromorphic computing and other approaches to the foundations of intelligence. This is a future research direction; we are not announcing hardware or research results.

IIIHuman–machine interfacesLonger-term ambition

New ways for people and technology to work together, including an interest in brain–computer interfaces. Any future work here must begin with appropriate expertise and evidence.

03How we build

A long horizon.
A clear next step.

  1. Earn the claim.

    Make it clear what works today, what we’re building, and what remains an ambition.

  2. Build for people.

    Useful systems should respect the people using them, their choices, and their private information.

  3. Go deep.

    Choose problems that matter. Learn the foundations. Keep improving the work.
