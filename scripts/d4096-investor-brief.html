D4096 / Design-stage instrument

4,096 points
of contact.

Rexzen is designing a non-invasive EEG array that samples each scalp electrode independently and rejects windows without enough usable coverage.

Current status: geometry and digital tests only, plus public-data reanalysis. No D4096 hardware, wearable performance, speech decoding or superiority claim.

01 / Verified in software

Test the data path.

480,000 synthetic packets round-tripped bit-exactly. In the fault simulation, evenly dispersed loss could clear both thresholds with 4,000 usable sites; the same loss clustered in one sector was rejected.

02 / Investment thesis

Prove that fit matters.

In our reanalysis of one published EEG session, more channels did not consistently help. Test the first use with neurotechnology labs and device teams before betting on a consumer wearable.

03 / Where capital buys evidence

Three prototype gates.

D0 · Next

64-contact bench coupon. Measure stability, noise and neighboring shorts without on-body acquisition.

D1

512-channel phantom build. Measure reference and timing stability; compare known source locations.

D2 / D3

4,096 lanes and a matched study. Verify acquisition, then independently clear human safety and compare against a lab baseline before a performance claim.
